Scenic

Local scene planning software made by some friends at Squid Productions to help you explain the vision before the shoot.

Build a set, block your talent, place your cameras, and shape the light. Saved on your computer.

Version 1.4.21 · Free for Mac and Windows

Download for MacDownload for Windows

Mac: Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 13.3 or later. Windows: Windows 10/11, 64-bit (x64). One complete offline .exe installer; no separate runtime downloads.

The Mac app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Windows is not code-signed and may show a SmartScreen prompt.

The space before the shot.

  • Decorate sets with searchable props, furnishings, doors, windows, and lights.
  • Plan in feet and inches. Build and group room walls; snap doors and windows into place.
  • Box-select scene items to move them together, and plan camera coverage using lens and sensor size.
  • Record movement marks for talent, cameras, and lights, then play the whole scene.
  • Study sun direction by shoot location, date, and time; preview RGB lights and window curtains.
  • Keep multiple productions and export maps, shot lists, and portable project backups.
  • Import Production Desk shot-list CSVs, choose scenes, link cameras, check coverage, and reimport updated lists without duplicates.
  • Browse numbered scenes with previews in a separate window, and resize the top toolbar for more workspace.
  • Review release notes before updating, and reopen recent changes from What’s New.

Projects stay on your device. No account or subscription. Lighting previews are an artistic 2D approximation.

Use Scenic → Check for Updates for new releases. Updating preserves your productions and scenes.
