Scenic 1.4.19 (24) — iPhone and iPad beta

Drag cameras, talent, lights, props, set items and room shapes from Items onto Scene map. On touch screens, hold briefly, then drag; swipe without holding to scroll. Click/tap an item and then the map still works. A straight-wall drag places its start; choose its endpoint to finish.

Check placement after zooming and panning, inch snapping, repeated drops, Undo/Redo, cancelled drops outside the map, rotation and relaunch. On iPad, check portrait drawers and landscape with Keep Scenes and Items on the left both enabled and disabled. Category navigation should keep Items available, and Properties should edit the dropped object.

The selected panel states, scene selector position and default landscape setting remain included. Load Demo opens The Last Cue as a separate fictional offline production. Repeat Load Demo after editing to confirm your changes remain.

Please verify existing productions, map/shot navigation, Save as, Share and Import project with portable .scenic files. No account or network is required to edit.
