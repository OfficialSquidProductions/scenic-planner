  • Add compact upright lighting flags, silver/gold/white/black flex fills, negative fill, reversible bounce boards and diffusion. Flag corners scale uniformly; side stretching and independent width/height edits are disabled while existing project dimensions remain intact.
  • Preview fixture-class distance falloff, Fresnel focus, source width, angle-dependent bounce, warm gold reflection, diffusion and absorption. New Studio Lights enable the approximation; existing lights retain their saved preview until enabled. Manufacturer references and assumptions are available in Properties. This is a relative 2D planning preview, not calibrated lux or exposure.
  • Open fixture barn doors outward and adjust each leaf independently to shape the depicted beam. Top and bottom doors affect the approximate throughput; left and right appear in the overhead chart.
  • Use Settings → Scene lighting layouts to enable independent lighting per camera or shot. New scenes default to shared lighting; turning the setting off restores the shared plan and retains saved variants. Export a selected plan or a scene-shot multipage PDF.
  • Draw curved walls with endpoint and bend handles, shape floor outlines with corner/edge points, and move complete shapes by their bodies. Placement-locked floors remain selectable so they can be unlocked in Properties. Sun arrows show the incoming-light direction.
  • Keep complex lighting previews responsive with bounded tracing and cached field rendering while preserving traced shadows. Flags retain a visible W.I.P. warning.
