Scenic 1.4.27

- Copy selected scene items with Command+C and paste with Command+V on Mac (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V on Windows). Copy a built environment into a later existing scene at the same positions while preserving its existing objects.
- Pasted items are independent copies with fresh IDs. Preserve relative layout, editable properties, floor/reference images, walls and groups, attached doors/windows, camera settings, movement marks and supported shot links. Repeated pastes are offset; normal text-field copy and paste remain available.
- Right-click a scene and choose Duplicate scene to reuse its complete constructed environment with independent object and shot IDs. Both paste and scene duplication support Undo/Redo and portable save/reopen.
