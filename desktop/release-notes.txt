Scenic 1.4.28

- Copy selected scene items with Ctrl+C and paste with Ctrl+V, including across existing scenes. Preserve the source environment, destination objects, layout, images, grouping, openings, camera settings and movement marks. Copies are independently editable.
- Right-click a scene and choose Duplicate scene to reuse its environment. Paste and scene duplication support Undo/Redo and portable save/reopen; normal text-field and secondary-window copy/paste remain available.
- Import complete scene.shot identifiers into their numbered scenes, preserving descriptions, Script coverage, camera links and existing supported numbering formats.
